Official rules

  1. Organiser. The challenge is run by TiffinBreak.com.
  2. Eligibility. Open to individuals aged 18 or older in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, India and the United Arab Emirates. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. People involved in running the challenge and their households cannot enter.
  3. Entry period. Each monthly challenge opens on the first day of the month and closes at 11:59 pm Eastern Time on its last day.
  4. How to enter. Submit the form above with a link to an original photo of your tiffin and the recipe. One entry per person per month. No purchase is necessary.
  5. Judging. This is a contest of skill. A panel chosen by TiffinBreak scores every eligible entry against the criteria above. Judges' decisions are final.
  6. Prizes. Three prizes per month, as listed on this page. Prizes are not allocated by country or region, cannot be exchanged for cash except at the organiser's discretion, and may be replaced by a prize of equal or greater value.
  7. Winners. Winners are contacted by email within 10 days of the closing date and must reply within 14 days or another winner may be chosen. Canadian residents must correctly answer a time-limited skill-testing question before receiving a prize.
  8. Your content. You keep ownership of your photo and recipe. By entering, you allow TiffinBreak to publish your entry, name and city on this website, its newsletter and its social channels, with credit.
  9. Privacy. We use your details only to run the challenge and contact you about it. See the privacy policy.
  10. General. TiffinBreak may disqualify entries that are not original, break these rules or contain inappropriate content, and may change or cancel a challenge if something outside its control affects it.

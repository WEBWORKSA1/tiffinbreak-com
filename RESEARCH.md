# TiffinBreak.com: concept research

## The decision

**Build a tiffin matchmaker that uses recipes and tools as its traffic engine.** Not a recipe blog (AdSense-only food blogs need millions of pageviews), and not a delivery marketplace (inventory, payments and logistics). TiffinBreak sells qualified introductions to tiffin kitchens, and funds the content with AdSense, sponsorships and reader support.

The domain reads two ways, and the site uses both: "tiffin break" is the South Asian school/work lunch break (pack a tiffin), and "tiffin" is the subscription meal (eat one, or cook them for others).

## Why this model wins (data)

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Demand is growing in the diaspora | CBC (Toronto) links rising tiffin demand to return-to-office, grocery prices and appetite for regional food. North Shore News reports subscription tiffins spreading into smaller Canadian towns. |
| Supply is in glut | A Toronto tiffin-marketplace co-founder told reporters "there are more sellers than customers"; one GTA marketplace lists 40 to 60 sellers. Kitchens need customers more than customers need kitchens, so **kitchens pay for leads**. |
| Kitchen unit economics support a lead fee | Weekday lunch plans start around CA$160/month (Urban Eats). Home tiffin gross margins run 40 to 50%; aggregator apps take 15 to 20% commission (DineOpen). A flat CA$10 to CA$15 per qualified lead is cheaper than a commission on a CA$160+ monthly subscriber who stays for months. |
| Market size | India's home-cooked meal delivery market is estimated at about US$3.5B (2024) with 20 to 25% CAGR (NextLeap analysis); tiffin services grow 15 to 20% a year (DineOpen). |
| Supply-side search intent | "How to start a tiffin service" content and videos are abundant and recent, so home cooks are actively searching. That feeds kitchen listings and B2B partner leads (software, packaging, licensing). |
| Planner gap | Mealime, a popular free meal planner, shuts down on 21 October 2026. A free tiffin-specific planner fills a gap. |

## Revenue scenario (assumptions are explicit; this is a model, not a forecast)

At 50,000 visits a month, roughly two-thirds from Canada, the US and the UK:

| Stream | Price | Assumption | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer match leads | CA$12 per routed lead | 1.2% of visits submit (600); 60% routable to a paying kitchen (360) | CA$4,320 |
| Office and bulk quotes | CA$60 per qualified quote | 15 per month | CA$900 |
| Featured / Spotlight listings | CA$39 / CA$99 per month | 30 Featured + 5 Spotlight | CA$1,665 |
| AdSense | RPM about US$4 blended | 90,000 pageviews | about CA$500 |
| Sponsorship, newsletter, challenge | | 1 to 2 small sponsors | CA$500 |
| **Total** | | | **about CA$7,900** |

Lead generation is roughly 80% of revenue. AdSense is the subsidy, not the business. The bold path: run paid search on "tiffin service + city" in the GTA once kitchens are signed, because a CA$12 lead that costs CA$4 to acquire is arbitrage.

## Gaps TiffinBreak exploits

1. Nobody combines **finding** a tiffin, **packing** one and **starting** a tiffin kitchen. Each competitor does one.
2. No recipe site says **how long a dish keeps in a tin** and how to pack it hot or cold. Every TiffinBreak recipe does.
3. Jain and no-onion-garlic tiffins are under-served by big platforms.
4. Regulatory guidance for home tiffin kitchens is scattered across dozens of government sites. One table by country is linkable, evergreen content.

## Trademark check

Searches found registered or applied marks containing "TIFFIN" in restaurant/catering class 43 (for example TIFFIN INDIAN CUISINE, TIFFIN BOX, and TIFFIN EXPRESS in India) but no "TIFFIN BREAK" mark in the results reviewed. The site uses "tiffin" and "tiffin break" descriptively, uses no third-party logos, and carries a non-affiliation disclosure on every page. Before filing a TiffinBreak mark of your own, run formal searches at CIPO, USPTO and IP India.

## Legal notes

- **Contests:** the monthly challenge is skill-based and judged, with prizes not allocated by region. Québec regulates publicity contests through the RACJ; contests open only to Québec or allocated by region can require registration and duties. Keep the challenge international with unallocated prizes, or get advice before a Québec-only promotion.
- **Food registration rules** are summarised in the start-a-tiffin-business guide with links to each authority.

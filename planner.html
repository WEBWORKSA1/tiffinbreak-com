    Too busy to cook at all this week? Get matched with a tiffin kitchen.

    How to use a tiffin plan

    Cook the longest recipe on Sunday and pick dishes that share ingredients across the week: the same tempering spices, one bunch of coriander, one bag of mixed vegetables. Rice-based tiffins are safest made fresh in the morning or cooled fast and refrigerated, so put them on days you have the most time. Breads such as thepla and paratha can be cooked ahead and keep best for the end of the week.

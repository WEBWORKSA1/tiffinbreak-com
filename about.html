About TiffinBreak

A proper midday meal, made the way you like it, is one of the small things that makes a working day or a school day go well. TiffinBreak exists to make that easier, whether you eat a tiffin, pack one, or cook them for other people.

What we do

  • Match people with tiffin kitchens. You tell us your city, diet, meals and budget; we introduce you to up to three kitchens that fit.
  • Publish tiffin recipes and tools. Recipes written for the tin, a weekly planner, and calculators for cost, safety and business planning.
  • Help home cooks build tiffin businesses. Guides to registration by country, pricing, menus and finding customers, plus listings that bring them customers.

How we make money

We are paid by tiffin kitchens for listings and introductions, by advertisers through Google AdSense and labelled sponsorships, and by readers who choose to support us. Customers never pay us, and payment never changes the facts in a guide or the safety advice in a recipe.

How we choose kitchens

We ask every kitchen for its food registration or permit, a written menu and a trial price, and we remove kitchens that customers report for repeated problems. We are a matching service, not a food business: kitchens are independent and responsible for their own food.

How we write

Recipes and guides are original. Facts and figures link to their sources, and safety advice follows public food-safety guidance. Read our editorial policy.

Independence

TiffinBreak.com is an independent website. It is not connected to any restaurant, caterer, tiffin service or brand with a similar name. See the trademark and copyright disclosure.

Privacy policy

Last updated {{ site.time | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}

This policy explains what information TiffinBreak.com ("TiffinBreak", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and its forms.

Information you give us

When you use a form (tiffin match request, office quote, kitchen listing, newsletter, contact, pledge, challenge entry, job application or partnership enquiry) we receive what you type, such as your name, email, phone number, city and preferences. We also record technical context that helps us handle the request: the page you submitted from, the first page you visited, the referring site, any campaign tags in the link (UTM parameters), the time, and for match requests a lead score calculated from your answers.

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-processing service.

How we use it

  • To do what you asked: match you with kitchens, prepare a quote, list your kitchen, send the newsletter, run the challenge, reply to messages.
  • Match and quote requests: with your consent, we share your request and contact details with up to three tiffin kitchens (or, for office quotes, suitable kitchens) so they can contact you. Those kitchens are independent businesses and handle your details under their own policies.
  • To improve the site and prevent spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and local storage

We store a few small items in your browser's local storage: your cookie-notice choice, your weekly tiffin plan, and how you first arrived at the site (landing page, referrer and campaign tags). These stay on your device until you clear them.

Advertising. This site uses Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads are used.

Third-party content

  • YouTube: videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player. Thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers. Once a video plays, Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for as long as we need them to provide the service and for our records, and no longer than 24 months for match requests that did not lead to further contact. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, withdraw consent, or unsubscribe, by sending a privacy request through the contact form. Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, Québec's Law 25, the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or California law) you may have additional rights; we honour them.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and challenge entries must come from people aged 18 or older.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Six questions to ask any tiffin kitchen

  1. Are you registered with the local food authority? Ask for the registration or permit number, not a promise.
  2. What does one tiffin contain? Count the rotis, the grams of rice and the number of sabzi or curry tins.
  3. How does the menu rotate? A fixed weekly rotation is normal; ask to see next week's.
  4. What's the pause and cancel rule? Good kitchens let you pause with a day's notice.
  5. When does it arrive, and in what? Steel tins or sealed containers, and a delivery window you can live with.
  6. Can I try before I subscribe? A paid trial meal or trial week is the norm.

## The four options at a glance

| Option | What arrives | Typical price | Your effort | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiffin service | Hot or same-day home-style meals, daily | Canada from about CA$160 a month for weekday lunch; India ₹2,400 to ₹4,000 a month | None | People who want daily home food and a fixed routine |
| Prepared-meal delivery | Chilled chef-made meals, weekly, heat in minutes | About US$12 to US$15 a meal on large US platforms | Reheat | Busy people who want variety and a microwave is available |
| Home-cook marketplace | Dishes from local home cooks, ordered ahead | Dishes from about US$7; weekly plans from about US$54 | Reheat | Trying many cuisines without a subscription |
| Meal kit | Pre-portioned ingredients and recipe cards | Varies widely with heavy first-box discounts | Cook every meal | People who enjoy cooking but hate planning and shopping |

## Tiffin service

**Pros:** food cooked that day by someone who cooks the way your family does; regional and diet-specific options (Jain, no onion-garlic, halal) that big platforms rarely offer; no cooking or washing up; usually the cheapest per meal in cities with many kitchens.

**Cons:** a fixed rotation means less choice day to day; delivery windows can be tight; quality depends heavily on the individual kitchen.

## Prepared-meal delivery

Chef-made meal platforms deliver a week of chilled meals at once. You choose from a large menu and reheat for two or three minutes. Most let you skip or pause weekly.

**Pros:** big choice, consistent quality, flexible weeks. **Cons:** meals are chilled rather than freshly cooked that day, regional Indian home cooking is thin on most menus, and per-meal prices are usually above a tiffin plan's.

## Home-cook marketplaces

Marketplaces connect you with local home cooks, often with a wide range of immigrant home cooking. Some now offer weekly "shared personal chef" plans.

**Pros:** variety and authenticity, no long subscription. **Cons:** coverage is limited to certain cities, quality varies by cook, and meals typically arrive refrigerated for reheating.

## Meal kits

Meal kits send ingredients and recipe cards; you do the cooking.

**Pros:** you learn to cook, no planning or shopping. **Cons:** you still cook and clean every day, which is the part most people wanted to stop doing; regular prices are much higher than first-box offers suggest.

## Which one should you choose?

- **You want home food every weekday and no decisions:** a tiffin service.
- **You want a different cuisine every day and have a microwave:** prepared-meal delivery or a home-cook marketplace.

How matching works

  1. Tell us what you eat. City, diet, meals, budget and when you want to start. It takes two minutes.
  2. We pick up to three kitchens. Only kitchens that deliver to your area and cook your diet.
  3. They contact you with menus and a trial. You choose, and you pay the kitchen directly. Never us.

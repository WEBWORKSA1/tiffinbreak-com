Find a tiffin service that cooks the way you eat

Two minutes of questions. Then up to three kitchens that deliver to your area contact you with their menu, price and a trial offer.

Start my request

  1. Tell us what you eat. Jain, no onion-garlic, halal, vegan, high-protein: be as specific as you like.
  2. We match your request. We look for kitchens that cover your postcode and cook your diet, and send your request to no more than three.
  3. Kitchens reply {{ "within one business day" }}. Most offer a paid trial meal or trial week before a monthly plan.

Why people use TiffinBreak

  • Students and newcomers who want food that tastes like home without a kitchen of their own.
  • Professionals who have stopped pretending they will meal-prep on Sunday.
  • New parents and seniors who need dependable, gentle food on weekdays.
  • Families who want dinner handled on the busiest nights.

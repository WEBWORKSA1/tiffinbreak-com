Frequently asked questions

For customers

Is TiffinBreak free for customers?

Yes. Kitchens pay for listings and introductions. You pay the kitchen you choose directly, at its own price, and nothing is added by us.

What is a tiffin service?

A subscription for home-style meals, usually lunch, dinner or both, delivered on set days for a weekly or monthly price. Most run a rotating menu and offer a trial first.

How much does a tiffin service cost?

In Canada weekday lunch plans commonly start around CA$160 a month; in India lunch-only plans commonly run ₹2,400 to ₹4,000 a month. Each city guide lists typical ranges, and the cost calculator compares a plan with cooking and takeout.

What happens after I send a match request?

We look for kitchens that deliver to your area and cook your diet, and share your request with up to three of them. They contact you, usually within one business day, with a menu, a price and a trial offer. If we cannot cover your area yet, we tell you.

Do you check the kitchens?

We ask every kitchen for its food registration or permit, a written menu and a trial price, and remove kitchens customers report for repeated problems. Kitchens are independent businesses and responsible for their food.

How long does a packed tiffin stay safe?

Perishable food should not sit at room temperature for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 32°C (90°F). A preheated insulated tin or two ice packs keeps a tiffin safe for a typical morning. Try the safety checker.

For kitchens

What does a listing cost?

A basic listing is free. Matched leads, Featured and Spotlight plans are paid; see list your kitchen.

Do I need to be registered?

You need to follow your local food rules. We list kitchens that are registered and those clearly working towards registration, and we show which is which. See rules by country.

Everything else

How does the monthly challenge work?

A new theme each month, judged on skill by a panel, with three prizes. Rules are on the challenge page.

Can I advertise or sponsor?

Yes. See advertising and sponsorship.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only for what you asked for, such as sharing a match request with kitchens or sending the newsletter. We do not sell them. Read the privacy policy.

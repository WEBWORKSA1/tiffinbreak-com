Keep the tiffin tools free

TiffinBreak is independent. Ads and kitchen listings pay part of the bill; readers who chip in pay for the rest and decide how fast we grow.

Choose an amount

Send a pledge with the form and we reply with a secure payment link. Nothing is charged until you pay that link.

Where the money goes

  • Operations: hosting, tools and the forms that connect customers with kitchens.
  • Recipes: developing and testing new tiffin recipes, including regional and Jain cooking.
  • Prizes: the monthly challenge gift cards.
  • People: paying writers, photographers and city partners.
  • Promotion: reaching more families and more kitchens in new cities.

TiffinBreak is not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible. Supporters who opt in are thanked by first name in the Sunday note.

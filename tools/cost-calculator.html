How the comparison works

A month is 4.33 weeks. Home cooking is shown twice: groceries only, and groceries plus the value of the time you spend cooking and washing up. Time is counted per cooking session, not per person, because cooking for two takes only a little longer than cooking for one. The tiffin plan is the price you would pay a kitchen, and takeout is the average you spend on a bought lunch.

If the tiffin plan wins on cost-plus-time but not on cash, the honest answer is that you are buying back hours. That is often the right trade for students in exam season, new parents and anyone working long shifts.

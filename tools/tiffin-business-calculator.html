Tiffin business calculator

Most new tiffin kitchens underprice. Enter what a tiffin really costs you, and see whether the business pays you more than a part-time job would.

Fixed costs: registration, gas and electricity share, insurance, marketing, software. Delivery: fuel, a driver, or a courier.

Benchmarks to sanity-check your numbers

  • Food cost: aim for under 40% of your price. Home tiffin kitchens commonly report 40 to 50% gross margins.
  • Break-even: Indian home tiffin services often need about 25 to 30 regular customers to cover their costs.
  • Delivery: self-delivery within 3 to 5 km works at the start; a paid driver makes sense from roughly 30 customers.
  • Aggregator apps: commissions of 15 to 20% are common, so price for it or stay direct.

Benchmarks from published guides on starting a tiffin service, including DineOpen. Your local costs will differ.

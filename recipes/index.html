How we write tiffin recipes

Most recipes assume you eat straight from the stove. A tiffin waits four or five hours, gets carried, tilted and opened at room temperature. So every recipe here tells you three things other sites skip: how long it keeps and under what conditions, how to pack it (hot in a preheated insulated tin, or cold with ice packs), and which ingredients to adjust so it is still good at lunch. Timing guidance follows public food-safety advice; see our tiffin food safety guide.

Editorial policy

What we publish

Recipes written for the tiffin, practical guides for tiffin customers and kitchens, city guides, and tools. Every page should help a reader do something: choose, pack, cook, compare or decide.

Recipes

Recipes are original. Each states how long it keeps and how to pack it, using public food-safety guidance on time and temperature as the baseline. Where a dish carries extra risk (rice, eggs, poultry, sprouts, dairy) the recipe says so.

Facts, figures and rules

Prices, market figures and regulations come from published sources, which we link. Government and regulator pages are preferred for rules. City price ranges are labelled as indicative and are not quotes.

Tools and drafting

We use software tools, including AI writing assistants, to help draft and check content. Facts, figures and safety statements are checked against the sources we cite before publication.

Independence and sponsorship

Advertisers and listed kitchens do not see or approve guides before publication. Sponsored recipes and videos are labelled as sponsored. Payment never changes safety advice.

Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, tell us through the contact form (topic "Correction to a page"). We fix confirmed errors promptly and update the page date.
